# EPISODE 1: Part 1 — The Ground Reality (Sequence)

## Scene 1: The Sacred Morning
- **Setting:** 5:30 AM, Kerala.
- **Key Beats:** Azan sounds, mandatory prayer, mint tea (no powder), Grandpa's stillness, the loud firecracker in the backyard.

## Scene 2: The Muddy Paddy Field
- **Setting:** Raw, organic muddy field.
- **Key Beats:** Heavy duels, the dead bandicoot rat, Akhil arriving on an Activa with a kaikkott.

## Scene 3: The Father's Anxiety & First-Aid Fail
- **Setting:** Sideline of the field.
- **Key Beats:** Father's wedding rush, son's desire for the final, the sputtering first-aid spray, the realization that they need Adam.

## Scene 4: The Conversation of Dreams
- **Setting:** Jaisal's backyard/Grandpa's room.
- **Key Beats:** Zidhan finds Jaisal digging a grave for a wild boar. The debate over "Kaalappoott" vs "Tactics." The metaphor of Jaisal rising from the grave. Grandpa's vision of Subhash Chandra Bose.

## Scene 5: The House of Shafiq
- **Setting:** Shafiq's house.
- **Key Beats:** WhatsApp from Gulf-based Shafiq, the mother's worry ("kayyo kaalo odichu"), Adam's vibrating energy.

## Scene 6: The Tournament (First Half)
- **Setting:** Muddy ground, 0-0 score.
- **Key Beats:** Tactical foul, red card, the fight, the slow-motion lock of eyes between Jaisal and Zidhan, the trigger of the grandfather's watch.
