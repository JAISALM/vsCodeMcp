# JAISAL KUT - INTRO
## Final Script v2
**Duration:** 0:67 | Black & White | No Music | Halal Compliant

**LOOK:** Black & White cinematic, high contrast, fine grain. 24fps
**LIGHTING:** Single practical bare bulb top-down 45°. No fill. Hard shadow.
**SOUND:** Diegetic only. Room tone, chair scrape, bulb hum, breath, sigh, paper rustle.
**NAME LOCK:** JAISAL KUT

---

## CORE PHILOSOPHY
Empty room → nothing yet
Plants grow → stories / ideas
Plants wilt → reality / limitation / loneliness
Jaisal disappears → his ideas remain
നോക്കാം → I don't know if it will work. Let's see.

Papers → stories
Microphone → voice / politics
Cricket objects → sports
Impossible movement → fantasy

### 0:56 - 1:07 | REVOLT - JAISAL EXPLODES INTO IDEAS
**Shot 7 - MEDIUM WIDE**
Lens: 35mm
Action: The plants are now completely dead. The room is almost empty again. Jaisal remains seated at the table. He finishes speaking: നോക്കാം.. A brief silence. Then DOZENS of loose sheets of paper begin to float in from the darkness BEHIND him, drifting forward through the room. A MICROPHONE emerges from the dark and settles among the papers. Some papers drift back and VANISH into the darkness. JAISAL himself VANISHES — he is gone, replaced by the floating papers. Then a CRICKET BAT and CRICKET STUMPS fly into the light, and a round SOCCER BALL rolls in underneath them. One paper — with a simple curved smile line drawn on it — settles on TOP of the bat as a crude head. The objects are fragments of Jaisal's ideas. Mic = politics/voice. Cricket = sports. Papers = stories. Impossible movement = fantasy.
IMPORTANT: No fire, smoke, glowing particles, futuristic AI effects, holograms, or colorful effects. The impact comes entirely from paper, physical objects, motion, shadows and sound.
Dialogue MALAYALAM: CAMERA GUY - ഈ ചാനൽ നിന്ന് ആൾക്കാർക്ക് എന്തൊക്കെ എക്സ്പെക്ട് ചെയ്യാം?
JAISAL - പറഞ്ഞപോലെ... കഥകൾ, രാഷ്ട്രീയം, കായികം, പിന്നെ അല്ലറ ചില്ലറ fantasy... ഇതൊക്കെയാണ് ഉദ്ദേശിക്കുന്നത്.
*Small pause.*
JAISAL - നടക്കുമോ എന്നറിയില്ല...
*Looks at the strange objects.*
JAISAL - നോക്കാം.
CAMERA GUY - നോക്കാം.
Dialogue ENGLISH: CAMERA GUY - What can people expect from this channel?
JAISAL - As I said... stories, politics, sports, and a little bit of fantasy. That's what I'm aiming for.
*Small pause.*
JAISAL - I don't know if it'll work...
