## The real problems (ordered by damage)

### 1. Jaisal's arc is a sermon
He is wrong from frame 1 (blames the system) and right at the end. "I was wrong" only lands if he was **right first**. Give him one genuine 2026 win — a tactical win that WORKS (the team plays his system, they are winning) — and then the system betrays him: the win brings the fight, the brawl, Adam's injury. A system that works AND fails is what makes "you can't change the system, you adapt" land. As written he's a guilt character, not an arc.

**The contradiction underneath:** the 1950 half proves tactics WORK (the X, the seed board, load-balanced geometry beat Brazil 3-2). The 2026 half says tactics ALONE don't. The film must reconcile on screen — the Uruguay monologue tries ("you adapt… basics") but it's a line, not a scene. Same fix: the 2026 win that betrays him. The line between the halves: **tactics cover the game; they don't cover the world** (the fight is off-pitch).

### 2. The family dimension is buried
The deepest idea — the trip is ANCESTRAL, not personal (faces-echo; little Abu Bakar; Grandpa's stillness = he is IN there, has been all his life) — lives in three fragments. The audience has to assemble it. One line in Scene 1 lands it: Grandpa isn't just dreaming — he is **in there**. Beat: the watch has done this before; the family has carried it. (You write the line; the beat is "again" — "the same dream, Jaisalu. I've been in it since before you were born.")

### 3. The cable trick works by everyone being too relieved
Zidhan burns the Federation's form, sends a duplicate, the postmaster is "too relieved to check," FIFA says "it is the only one we have." A smart audience asks: why is a fake duplicate accepted? The script's answer is everyone's relief — that's COMEDY logic in a serious story. **The fix is already in the script, unconnected:** the fire (the lamp, the oil) + "Counter 11 closed until Thursday." Connect them: the fire kills the wire room / the outgoing tray — the Federation's cable is PHYSICALLY LOST, so the unsigned duplicate is literally the only record that exists. Then it's not "everyone too relieved to check" — it's "the truth was destroyed; this is all that's left." A plot engine, not a wish. The official's line ("If India is coming, India will come") lands HARDER when the wire is actually dead.

### 4. The Scene 23 reveal line has a bug
"faces we know from black and white: Anto, Gopu, Babu, Ragu, **Adam**" — but Adam has NO 1950 echo (11 players echo 11 kids; Adam is the 12th, the outsider, the Shafiq line — 2026 only). Listing him among the B&W faces is a continuity bug. **The fix is better than the fix: make the exception the point.** Everyone in the heap we know from black and white — EXCEPT Adam, who was never there, who wasn't in the trip, who took the foul on the ground. "Anto, Gopu, Babu, Ragu — and Adam, who was never there." The reveal is about RETURN; the one face that DIDN'T travel is the one who STAYS. The exception is the emotional beat.

### 5. The runtime is a 40-minute lie
24 core + 8 optional ≈ 35–40 min. Festival shorts are 15 (some 30). The film's SPINE is 10 scenes: Sacred Morning → The Ground → Boar Grave → The Shift → Shore/MARJAN → The Cable → Maracanã → The Spin → The Watch. ≈ 25 min. **Cut the ⊕ scenes (they're marked for this) and cut 4 of the 5 recruitment vignettes** — keep the 3 that pay a match-cut (fisherman=Antappan, card player=Thomas, market manager=Moosa), cut the other 2 (husk beaters, coir makers — their faces are in the heap; they don't need solo scenes). The film gets tighter; the echo stays intact.
