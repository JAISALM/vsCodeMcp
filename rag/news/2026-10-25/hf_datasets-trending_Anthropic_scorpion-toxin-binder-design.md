# Anthropic/scorpion-toxin-binder-design


	
		
	
	
		Scorpion toxin binder design: data release
	

Claude designed proteins de novo to bind five scorpion toxins: Cn2, Ts1, AaH II, AaH I, and LqhIII. Two contract research organizations measured binding by surface plasmon resonance (SPR): Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience. Seventeen designs are binders: seven against Cn2, four against AaH II, four against AaH I, two against LqhIII, and none against Ts1.
