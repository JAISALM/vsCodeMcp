# EPISODE 1: Part 1 — The Ground Reality (Full Script)

## Scene 1: The Sacred Morning
**(Visual: The screen is dark. We hear the distant, hauntingly beautiful sound of the Azan. As the light of dawn breaks, we see Jaisal waking from a nap. He performs his mandatory morning prayer in a quiet, spiritual atmosphere.)**

**Action:** Jaisal moves to the kitchen. He opens the fridge, plucks a few fresh, aromatic mint leaves, and steeps them in hot water. No tea powder—just pure, fragrant mint tea. 

**(Jaisal enters Grandpa's room. Abu Bakar is sitting perfectly still, his watch glinting on his wrist in the soft light.)**

**Jaisal:** (Softly, first call) "Grandpa?"
**(No response. Abu Bakar is staring into space, lost in his own world.)**
**Jaisal:** (A second call, slower, with a smile) "Grandpa... I brought your tea."
**(Grandpa remains in his "stillness." Jaisal doesn't panic; he simply smiles and places the tea on the table with care.)**

**(SUDDENLY: A loud, sharp CRACK of a firecracker echoes from the backyard. Jaisal jumps slightly and rushes toward the backyard—his *parambu*—passing through the scent of wet earth and coconut trees.)**

---

## Scene 2: The Transition & The Muddy Field
**(Cinematic Transition: Jaisal is running in the backyard. As the camera follows him, a massive coconut tree trunk enters the frame from the right, momentarily blocking the view. As the camera slides past the tree, we "wipe" from the backyard into the Training Ground.)**

**(Visual: A raw, organic muddy paddy field. Kids (11-12 years old) are locked in heavy duels, their jerseys stained with mud and sweat. They are playing intensely on one side of the pitch, while the center is a "no-go zone.")**

**(In the center of the pitch, a bandicoot rat lies dead. The ball rolls to a stop near it.)**

**(The sound of an Activa engine humming. Akhil (18-20) zips into the field, a *kaikkott* (Kerala shovel) balanced in one hand. Zidhan stands on the sideline, catches Akhil's eye, and uses a series of urgent gestures—pointing to the rat and then to his watch.)**

**Zidhan:** (Gesturing urgency) "Akhil! Get in here and clear this mess, fast!"
**(Akhil hops off the Activa, scoops the rat out of the mud with one swift motion, and tosses it aside. The field is cleared, and the game restarts with a roar.)**

---

## Scene 3: The Father's Anxiety & The First-Aid Fail
**(Visual: A father stands by the field, checking his watch every few seconds. He looks stressed.)**

**Father:** (To Akhil, as Akhil finishes burying the rat) "Akhil, we really have to head out soon. We’re going to the other district for the wedding, and we need to be there by noon!"
**Akhil:** (Smiling) "Almost done, Uncle! Just a few more seconds."

**(Suddenly, a shout from the pitch. A kid has gone down with a twisted ankle. Zidhan screams from the sideline.)**

**Zidhan:** "Akhil! First aid! Now!"
**(Akhil rushes over and presses the spray. *Pssssst... psss...* The spray sputters, almost empty. Akhil looks at the nozzle with a frustrated face.)**

**Akhil:** (Sighing) "It's almost empty..."

**(The father gathers the injured kid and his own son, guiding them toward the exit.)**

**Father:** "Alright, let's get you both home. The injured one lives just nearby, so we can drop him off on our way to the wedding."

**(Zidhan and Akhil stand together, looking at the remaining players.)**

**Akhil:** "Zidhan-ikka, we are short. We really need another player... someone who can hold our legs well. Someone with a bit of strength."
**Zidhan:** "Who else is there at this hour?"
**Akhil:** "Jaisalkan's brother... Adam. He's the one."
**Zidhan:** (Nodding) "Is he alright? Is he ready?"
**Akhil:** "He's more than alright. He's dying to get back on the pitch."

---

## Scene 4: The Conversation of Dreams
**(Visual: Zidhan arrives at Jaisal's house. He stops at the window, looking inside. He sees Grandpa in his stillness.)**

**Zidhan:** (To himself) "He's dreaming again..."

**(Zidhan hears a rhythmic scratching sound coming from behind the house. He walks toward the backyard and finds Jaisal crouching in a hole, digging a grave. Beside the hole lies a dead wild boar.)**

**Zidhan:** (Confused) "What are you doing down there, Jaisal? Why the grave?"
**Jaisal:** (Looking up) "This wild boar got caught in the cracker trap. The dogs kept digging it up, so I'm digging a bigger one this time."
**(Jaisal pauses, looking at Zidhan with a philosophical glint.)**
**Jaisal:** "You know, Zidhan... dogs only know how to dig up the past things."
**(Zidhan laughs, realizing Jaisal is calling him a 'dog' for his obsession with previous failures.)**

**Zidhan:** (Standing above him) "Fair enough. But listen—we need Adam for the final. Can you bring him?"
**Jaisal:** (Hesitating) "I don't know... maybe. Why the sudden rush? Is the team not ready?"
**Zidhan:** "The 'Rockstar Boys' are playing us. They are bigger and stronger. Tactics alone won't work; we need *Kaalappoott*. We need Adam's height to hold the line. Plus... if we win this, we can actually afford to keep the academy open. We might even buy a full bottle of first-aid spray!"

**(Jaisal looks at Zidhan's desperate face. He reaches his hand up from the grave.)**

**Jaisal:** "Alright... *nokkaam* (let's see). I'll try to bring him. Now, give me a hand to get out of here."
**(Zidhan pulls him up. Jaisal rises from the grave—a metaphor for returning to his passion.)**

**(Transition: Jaisal walks to the washbasin, washing his muddy hands and feet. As he grabs the towel, he looks over at Grandpa, who is now without his watch—it is resting on the table.)**

**Jaisal:** "You've been dreaming a lot lately, Grandpa."
**Grandpa:** (Softly) "Our dream is not to destroy others, Jaisalu... it is to live happily within them."
**Jaisal:** "What are you dreaming about now? My mother?"
**Grandpa:** "Many people dream about your mother. But right now, I am dreaming that no one in my family has ever left me. And I can see Subhash Chandra Bose... on the magnificent ship my father used to own."
**Jaisal:** "I think I'll try that too. A little time travel to escape this reality."

---

## Scene 5: The House of Shafiq
**(Visual: Jaisal checks his phone—a WhatsApp from Shafiq in the Gulf: "Bring him! He's ready!")**

**(Inside, Jaisal meets Shafiq's wife and the young boy, Adam.)**

**Shafiq's Wife:** (Skeptical) "*Aarelum kayyo kaalo odichu ennum paranjond ingott vannaal athre ullu* (If you just come here saying a hand or a leg might break, that's all it is!)."
**Adam:** (Bouncing with excitement) "But Amma! I want to play! I *have* to play!"
**Jaisal:** (Smiling) "Don't worry. We'll take care of him. He's the missing piece Zidhan needs."

---

## Scene 6: The Tournament (The First Half)
**(Visual: The muddy ground. 0-0 score. Jaisal is in the gallery (the observer); Zidhan is on the sideline (the manager).)**

**(The kids are battling. A player makes a sharp, calculated move—a tactical foul to save a goal.)**

**Referee:** (Loud whistle) "Yellow Card!"

**(The foul triggers a fight. The kids start shoving and shouting—typical 5's football chaos. The referee is struggling to keep control.)**

**(The world slows down. Jaisal (gallery) and Zidhan (sideline) lock eyes. Jaisal sees the "noise" and the fight; Zidhan sees the "logic" of the foul.)**

**(Jaisal stands up. He slowly brings his wrist up, looks at the grandfather's watch, and adjusts the dial.)**

**Jaisal:** (A whisper) "Let's change the history."

**(The colors of Kerala begin to fade into a cinematic Black & White transition...)
