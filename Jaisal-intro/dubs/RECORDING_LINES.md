**How to record:** DaVinci Resolve → Edit page → position the playhead at the line's spot →
right-click → **Voice-Over Record** (or the mic icon in the timeline toolbar). Record while
watching the video so the line is timed to Jaisal's mouth. Project audio format = 48 kHz.
Headset as the default input. **Speak in MALAYALAM** (the English below is the meaning /
audience-subtitle text — perform the Malayalam).

**One voice:** **JAISAL** = your own voice (as-is).

**CAMERA GUY = MUTED.** The 4 black question cards ARE the questions (visual text only).
The audio under each card = ambient / background noise or a Jaisal line (NOT the camera
guy's voice). No RVC, no Midhun.
