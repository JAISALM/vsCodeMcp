# Jaisal Kut intro — per-shot descriptions for the H3 prompt model
# Each block is the --desc text for gen_h3_prompt.py. Refs are listed in the
# batch runner (gen_all_h3_prompts.py) in <Picture N> order.
# LOOK: Black & white, high contrast, fine film grain, 24fps. Single bare bulb
# top-down, hard shadow, everything outside the light pool fades to pure black.
# ONE character (Jaisal). NO music.

=== A | THE WALK IN (5s) ===
Shot 1 - WIDE STATIC, 35mm, eye level, locked camera (no move/push/pan/zoom).
The empty dark room: a simple wooden table, one chair, a single bare bulb
hanging directly above, the only light source. The bulb swings gently. A chair
scrapes across the floor. Jaisal enters from the darkness, drags the chair, and
sits down. He ends seated at the table. Black & white, high contrast, fine film
grain.

=== B | SETUP (3s) ===
Shot 2 - MEDIUM, 50mm, slight low angle, locked camera. Jaisal is seated at the
table. He settles, both hands coming to rest ON the table edge (visible above
the edge, NOT under it). Calm, still. Black & white, high contrast, fine film
grain.

=== C | THE HOOK - SLOW HEAD TWIST (camera node, ~10s) ===
Shot 3 - CLOSE UP, 85mm, eye level. Jaisal seated, bulb directly above his head.
Silence. His eyes shift first. His head begins to rotate SLOWLY 180 degrees
toward the camera while his body stays still and lit the same. The camera
rotates and pushes in at exactly the same rate. Black & white, high contrast,
fine film grain. (This shot uses the H3LocalCameraEditor node - azimuth 0 to 180.)

=== D | PERFECT REVERSE (7s) ===
Shot 4 - MEDIUM REVERSE, 50mm. Everything reverses perfectly: the head rotation
reverses, the camera rotation reverses, clothing motion reverses. We land on
ordinary seated Jaisal, hands on the table. The weird version was his mind.
Black & white, high contrast, fine film grain.

=== E | DREAM - PLANTS GROW (13s) ===
Shot 5 - MEDIUM to CLOSE push, 50mm to 85mm, slow push-in. Jaisal seated at the
table. Plants GROW (not fade in): one tiny stem pushes through the table
surface, another follows, a rose slowly opens, small plants populate the table.
The room is created by the story. Black & white, high contrast, fine film grain.

=== F | LIMIT - PLANTS DIE SLOW (18s) ===
Shot 6 - CLOSE UP, 85mm. Jaisal seated, breathes, looks at the plant. While he
admits limitation, a leaf falls, the plant bends, the rose closes, the room
empties slowly. No AI glow, no holograms - just limitation. Black & white, high
contrast, fine film grain.

=== G | REVOLT - VANISH BURST (3s) ===
Shot 7a - MEDIUM WIDE, 35mm. The plants are now completely dead, the room almost
empty. Jaisal remains seated. Then DOZENS of loose sheets of paper begin to float
in from the darkness behind him, drifting forward. A microphone emerges from the
dark and settles among the papers. Some papers drift back and vanish. JAISAL
himself VANISHES - he is gone, replaced by the floating papers. ABSENCE-FIRST:
the boy is NOT visible in the end frame, only the papers and mic. Black & white,
high contrast, fine film grain.

=== H | MIC + PAPER (3s) ===
Shot 7b - MEDIUM, 35mm. The floating papers and the microphone settle into a
calm arrangement in the light. A few papers drift. Black & white, high contrast,
fine film grain.

=== I | MIC ALONE (2.3s) ===
Shot 7c - MEDIUM, 35mm. The papers drift away and vanish into the darkness,
leaving only the microphone alone in the light. Black & white, high contrast,
fine film grain.

=== J | OBJECTS JOIN (3s) ===
Shot 7d - MEDIUM WIDE, 35mm. A cricket bat and cricket stumps fly into the
light, and a round soccer ball rolls in underneath them. One paper with a simple
curved smile line settles on top of the bat as a crude head. The objects are
fragments of Jaisal's ideas. No fire, smoke, glowing particles, holograms, or
colorful effects - only paper, physical objects, motion, shadows. Black & white,
high contrast, fine film grain.
