---
name: save-decision
description: Record a meaningful project decision in DECISIONS.md
---

A meaningful project decision was just made.

Capture it as a new entry at the top of project-memory/DECISIONS.md (continue the D-number sequence from the latest entry):

- Date and short title (D-number)
- Decision
- Why
- Alternatives considered
- Result (if known; otherwise "Pending")
- Status: Active

If the decision changes current state, also update project-memory/PROJECT_STATE.md (Current Decisions, Current Goal, or Current Workflow as appropriate).

Do not duplicate existing entries.

Keep entries concise.

When done, respond with a one-line confirmation of what was recorded.
