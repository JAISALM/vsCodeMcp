=====================================================================
KF3B — HEAD 180° HORROR (face over shoulder, looking at camera)
=====================================================================
Take this to ChatGPT and ask it to improve the wording. The GOAL:
- Reference image = a BACK VIEW of a young man seated at a wooden
  table (we see his back, shoulders, back of head, single bulb,
  pure black void, B&W).
- We want ONLY the head to twist around so his FACE is visible,
  turned over his shoulder, looking STRAIGHT AT THE CAMERA.
- The BODY / SHOULDERS stay in the back view (facing away).
- The result = a creepy "ghost turned around to look at you" look.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THE CURRENT PROMPT FAILS (over-rotates to ~270°):
- Saying "rotate 180 degrees" makes the model overshoot.
- A human head cannot physically turn 180°, so the model is
  confused and picks a sideways (270°) pose.
- FIX: describe the END STATE (face visible over the shoulder,
  looking at the camera), NOT the rotation amount.

=====================================================================
POSITIVE PROMPT (feed to Krea2 identity-edit, ref = back-view image)
=====================================================================
A black and white high-contrast cinematic photograph. A single young
man with dark wavy hair and a black shirt, seated at a wooden table
on a wooden chair, a single bare bulb hanging above, a pure black
void background. The camera is behind him (a back view) - we see his
back, his shoulders, and the back of his head.

His head is turned completely around over his shoulder, so his FACE is
fully visible and he is looking STRAIGHT AT THE CAMERA, his eyes
staring directly at the viewer. His face is facing the camera head-on
(not a side profile, not turned to the left or right). His body and
shoulders remain in the back view, facing away from the camera - only
his head is turned around. A slow, unsettling, horror look, like a
ghost turning around to look at you.

Everything else is exactly the same as the reference: the same wooden
table, the same chair, the same single bulb, the same pure black void,
the same black and white high-contrast lighting. There is ONLY ONE man
in the frame.

=====================================================================
NEGATIVE PROMPT
=====================================================================
two people, multiple people, duplicate, twin, mirror image, reflection,
second person, extra man, front view of the body, body turned around,
shoulders turned around, face in profile, side of the face, face
pointing sideways, face turned to the left, face turned to the right,
face looking away from the camera, face not visible, back of head only,
head over-rotated, 270 degree rotation, full body rotation, tilted
camera, dutch angle, wall, bright background, white background, color,
extra chair, objects on the table, clutter, grain, speckles

=====================================================================
KEY TIPS FOR CHATGPT / TUNING
=====================================================================
1. Describe the END STATE, not the rotation: "his face is visible over
   his shoulder, looking at the camera" - NOT "rotate 180 degrees".
2. Emphasize "face FACING THE CAMERA head-on" + negative "face in
   profile / pointing sideways" to stop the 270° overshoot.
3. Keep "body and shoulders stay in the back view" so only the head
   moves.
4. If it still overshoots: lower ref_boost (node 79) from 4 to 3 so
   the reference's back-of-head pose pulls less, and the face-around
   pose wins.
5. If the face is too small / hidden: add "his face is clearly visible,
   large in the frame, the focal point".
6. Try 3-4 different seeds - this pose is hard and seed-sensitive.
