# EPISODE 1 — Story Decisions

Story-specific decisions for Episode 1 ("The Sweetness of What Could Have Been").
Global/project-level decisions live in `project-memory\DECISIONS.md` (D129–D130 mirror
the entries below). **Read this before building any workflow for an Episode 1 scene.**

## Core concept
- **The bridge** between Imagination (Tactics/Emotion) and Acceptance (Physicality/Reality).
- Magical-realist framing: the grandfather's watch turns tragedy into a "sweet dream".
- Emotional goal: how Jaisal and Zidhan find happiness knowing they had their own
  tragedy — the sweetness of what could have been.

| Character | Role | Notes |

## Metaphors & motifs (locked)
- **The Watch** — trigger for the magical-realist jump (2026 → 1950).
- **The Mud** — raw, unpolished beauty of grassroots football.
- **The Grave** (boar grave) — digging down = digging up the past; Jaisal rising with
  Zidhan's help = his journey back into the academy.
- **The Fax** — magical correction to history: burning the post office in Madras so
  India actually reaches Brazil in 1950.
- **The Coconut Tree Wipe** — seamless frame-wipe from same-day home life to the
  training ground.
- **The Sputtering Spray** — nearly-empty first-aid spray = the academy's lack of
  resources; the tournament win is what funds it.

## Scene / beat decisions
- **The "Kaalappoott" Shift (D130):** first half uses a **Yellow Card + Fight** (NOT a
  Red Card) — Adam stays in the game; shows the messy, physical nature of fives football.
- **Slow-motion eye lock** between Jaisal and Zidhan in the tournament scene — the
  grandfather's watch is the trigger of the mind-trip.
- **Act I "The Bitter Present":** academy ground (harsh sun, Kalapoot game) +
  grandfather's room (golden hour, single beam of light, tick-tick-tick) — the watch
  is handed to Jaisal there.
- **1950 mind-trip:** Jaisal picks players by **Football IQ** (math, space,
  positioning) — NOT raw power.
- **The Lesson:** India loses the final to Brazil **3-1** — IQ dominates the game,
  Physicality wins the final.
- **The Return:** Jaisal comes back to 2026, accepts reality → the 5s team wins the
  tournament.

## Ideology setup (must be established in the beginning)
- Jaisal and Zidhan were both good players; lack of talent kept them from "reaching
  there" → Zidhan became materialistic, Jaisal emotional → Jaisal is no longer at the
  academy; Zidhan runs it alone.
- The 1950 "what-if" is also about the **football federation / football society** being
  the reason football (and Jaisal) never grew in India — not just Jaisal's absence.
