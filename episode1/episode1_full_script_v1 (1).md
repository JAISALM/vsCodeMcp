## 1. VISUAL & SOUND BIBLE

**2026 (Kerala):** full natural colour, wet green, red mud, harsh noon white. Handheld camera, close and sweaty. Everything a little too crowded.

**1950 (the mind-trip):** high-contrast black & white, golden-hour bloom, vignette edges dissolving into light. Locked-off or slow-gliding camera. **Colour survives in only three things:** the two graphic T-shirts (they drain slowly over the voyage), **fire** (gold), and **the watch** (a faint gold glint always).

**Sound rules (whole film):** no score, no needle-drop. Only what exists in the world: the Azan, the watch tick, wind, surf, oars, rigging, bare feet, coconut thuds, crowd claps, voices. The anthem is sung, never played.

**Anachronism rule:** Jaisal and Zidhan keep their modern words ("fax", "press", "load balancing"). The 1950 villagers translate them into their own life-language. That translation is the film's humour and its philosophy.

**Faces echo:** the 1950 villagers are played by the same faces as the 2026 kids. Jaisal recruits, without realising it, the children he already coaches around. The 360° spin reveals it.
